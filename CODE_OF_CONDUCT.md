# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good
intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and accept constructive feedback.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks,
publication of private information, and disruption of project spaces. Maintainers may edit
or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact described in `SECURITY.md`.
Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
